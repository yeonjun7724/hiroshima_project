Counted in Full

Four bugs, one mistake: when partial spatial overlap is treated as whole, the map still draws and the answer is still wrong.

네 개의 버그, 하나의 실수. 부분적인 공간 중첩을 전체로 취급하면 지도는 여전히 그려지고, 답은 여전히 틀린다.

The mistake, in one picture 한 장으로 보는 실수

Every defect found in this codebase reduces to the same operation: a spatial predicate that answers yes or no is used where the question was how much. 이 코드베이스에서 발견된 모든 결함은 같은 연산으로 환원된다. '얼마나'를 물어야 할 자리에 '예/아니오'로 답하는 공간 술어를 쓴 것이다.

The cell is 34 % inside the district. Both methods run without error. Only one is an estimate of anything.
격자는 34%만 행정동 안에 있다. 두 방법 모두 에러 없이 실행된다. 추정치라 할 수 있는 것은 하나뿐이다.

Findings, by magnitude 크기순 발견 사항

All four measured against the same data. Every one produces a running program, a rendered map, and a plausible number. 네 건 모두 동일 데이터로 측정했다. 하나도 빠짐없이 정상 실행되고, 지도가 그려지고, 그럴듯한 숫자를 낸다.

Binary intersects() for population

Finding 05 · a different mechanism

A district disappeared, and nobody noticed for six months

Seoul has 426 dongs and 425 distinct names. 신사동 exists in both 관악구 and 강남구. The lecture builds its population lookup with set_index("region_nm").to_dict() — a dictionary keyed on a non-unique key. One of the two survives. 서울은 426개 동에 이름은 425개다. 신사동이 관악구와 강남구에 각각 있다. 비고유 키로 dict를 만들면 하나만 살아남는다.

강남구 신사동

No exception. No warning. A district silently dropped out of an analysis about which districts are underserved — and it placed last, which is exactly where a genuinely underserved district would also appear. 예외도, 경고도 없다. '어느 동이 소외됐는가'를 묻는 분석에서 한 동이 조용히 빠졌다. 그리고 최하위에 놓였는데, 진짜로 소외된 동이 놓일 자리도 바로 거기다.

This one does not fit the overlap frame — it is identity, not geometry. Both belong to the same family: a lossy operation that returns a valid-looking result. 이 건은 중첩 프레임에 안 맞는다. 기하가 아니라 동일성의 문제다. 그러나 같은 계열이다 — 정보를 잃으면서 멀쩡해 보이는 결과를 돌려주는 연산.

Finding 04 is the one to open with, not the one to lean on. Its small error is the point: the same bug is nearly harmless on a mountain and severe in a dense block. Severity is a property of the terrain, not of the code. 04번은 시작용이지 기댈 곳이 아니다. 오차가 작다는 것 자체가 논점이다. 같은 버그가 산에서는 거의 무해하고 고밀 주거지에서는 심각하다. 심각도는 코드가 아니라 지형의 속성이다.

Abstract draft 초록 초안

Roughly 210 words. Trim to the CFP limit once it is known. 약 210단어. CFP 제한 확인 후 조정.

Counted in Full: How Partial Spatial Overlap Silently Breaks Accessibility Analysis

Six months ago I built a four-part course on public transport accessibility in Seoul, using GeoPandas, OSMnx and deck.gl to find districts underserved by bus and subway. Re-auditing it for this talk, I found that one of the districts had quietly fallen out of the analysis altogether — two dongs share the name 신사동, the population lookup was keyed on that name, and one of them ended up with no population, no score, and last place out of 427 rows describing 426 districts.

It was not alone. Four further defects all reduced to the same operation: a spatial predicate answering whether two geometries meet, used where the question was how much. Measured against the same data, they overstated affected population by 3.2 %, 23.1 %, 34.8 % and 51.7 %. None raised an exception. None distorted a map badly enough to notice.

Then I moved the three index weights the original had fixed at 0.33 without argument. Across 231 combinations, 24 districts entered the top five and seven reached first place. The published winner held rank one in 39 % of weightings; a district absent from the published result took first in 22 %.

Generated code makes plausible spatial analysis cheap to produce and no cheaper to judge. I will show the audit, the corrections, and why the same code that is nearly harmless on an empty Seoul hillside becomes dangerous on the inhabited slopes of Kure and Onomichi.
